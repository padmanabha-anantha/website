GOSSIP 2027

Workshop date January 5, 2027

ABOUT

Games, Observation, Secrets, Strategies, Information, Protocols (GOSSIP) workshop will be held at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru. It will be a pre-conference workshop for the Indian Conference on Logic and its Applications (ICLA 2027, 6 - 8 January).

Invited Speakers
  • Tim French, The University of Western Australia, Perth, Australia
  • Andreas Herzig, University of Tolouse, Tolouse, France
  • Hannes Leitgeb, Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München, Munich, Germany
  • R Ramanujam, Azim Premji University, Bengaluru, India

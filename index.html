Anantha Padmanabha

I am an assistant professor at IIT Madras, Chennai in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. I am broadly interested in applications of logic in computer science.

Before joining IIT Madras, I was an Assistant Professor at IIT Dharwad. I did my Post Doctoral research at the VALDA team at Ecole Normale Superieure, Paris and at the Institut de Recherche en Informatique Fondamentale, Paris. I did my PhD at the Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Chennai. During my Post-doctoral career, I was also a part-time faculty at the New York University (Paris campus).

I am also passionate about teaching and learning new things. Apart from research I like to solve puzzles.

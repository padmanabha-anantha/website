Research

Logic, databases & computation

I am broadly interested in applications of logic in computer science. My work focuses on the foundations of databases, modal logics, and decidable fragments of first-order modal logic.

I also study the interaction between logic and computation, including questions around query answering, games, and reasoning over systems with unboundedly many agents.

Selected publications

  • A Simple Algorithm for Consistent Query Answering under Primary Keys Diego Figueira, Anantha Padmanabha, Luc Segoufin and Cristina Sirangelo — Logical Methods in Computer Science, 2025

    This work proposes a simple fix-point algorithm for consistent query answering under primary key constraints and that works for all PTIME solvable self-join free boolean conjunctive queries (BCQs) and path BCQs. It also proves that there are some queries that involve self-joins that are solvable in PTIME, but not by the proposed algorithm.

    Read paper
  • A Dichotomy in the Complexity of Consistent Query Answering for Two Atom Queries With Self-Join Anantha Padmanabha, Luc Segoufin and Cristina Sirangelo — Proceedings of the ACM on Management of Data, 2024

    This paper proves the complexity dichotomy for consistent query answering over two-atom self-join queries.

    Read paper
  • Boundedness for Unions of Conjunctive Regular Path Queries over Simple Regular Expressions Diego Figueira, S. Krishna, Om Swostik Mishra and Anantha Padmanabha — KR, 2024

    This paper studies boundedness for unions of conjunctive regular path queries over various restricted regular expressions to get better complexity bounds.

    Read paper
  • Are bundles good deals for First-Order Modal Logic? Mo Liu, Anantha Padmanabha, R. Ramanujam and Yanjing Wang — Information and Computation, 2023

    This paper considers various bundled fragments of first-order modal logic and classifies them as decidable, undecidable, and those that lose the finite model property (but their decidability is not known).

    Read paper
  • A Decidable Fragment of First Order Modal Logic: Two Variable Term Modal Logic Anantha Padmanabha and R. Ramanujam — ACM Transactions on Computational Logic, 2023

    This paper studies the two variable fragment of term modal logic and proves that it is decidable.

    Read paper
  • Implicit quantification for modal reasoning in large games Ramit Das, Anantha Padmanabha and R. Ramanujam — Synthese, 2023

    The paper introduces a modal logic for reasoning about large anonymous games and equilibrium behavior using implicit quantification over players.

    Read paper
  • The Monodic Fragment of Propositional Term Modal Logic Anantha Padmanabha and R. Ramanujam — Studia Logica, 2019

    This paper studies the monodic fragment of propositional term modal logic and proves that it is decidable.
